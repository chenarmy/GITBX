## Subsetting note

The Alibaba PuHuiTi and HarmonyOS Sans SC files are character-coverage subsets
(GB2312 + Big5 level-1 hanzi, kana, Latin and common symbols). Glyphs outside
the subset fall back to the system font through the CSS font stack; the fonts
themselves are otherwise unmodified.
